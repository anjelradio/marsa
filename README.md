# 🪐 MARSA

### Explore Earth, Mars & the Moon

**MARSA** is an interactive web platform developed for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**, addressing the challenge *Permanent Moon Base Locations and Mars*.

The project connects planetary exploration with Earth-based analog environments, helping users discover and compare geographical, geological, and environmental characteristics across Earth, Mars, and the Moon.

## Deployment: [https://marsa-pearl.vercel.app/](https://marsa-pearl.vercel.app/)

## 🌌 About the Project

Exploring other worlds begins with understanding our own.

MARSA provides an immersive experience where users can navigate interactive planetary globes, select locations, and discover how environments on Earth resemble those found on Mars and the Moon.

By bringing together planetary imagery, scientific information, and geospatial visualization, MARSA makes planetary exploration more accessible, visual, and educational.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Interactive Planetary Exploration:** Navigate 3D globes of Earth, Mars, and the Moon.
- **Planetary Comparisons:** Explore similarities and differences between terrestrial and extraterrestrial environments.
- **Location-Based Insights:** Select geographical locations to discover environmental and geological information.
- **Scientific Data Visualization:** Present available planetary imagery, temperature information, terrain characteristics, and other relevant data.
- **Earth Analog Discovery:** Understand how environments on Earth can help scientists study conditions on other worlds.
- **Immersive Experience:** Modern, responsive interfaces designed to make space exploration engaging and intuitive.

## 🛰️ Scientific Data Sources

MARSA explores publicly available scientific resources from NASA and other space science organizations, including:

- **NASA Planetary Data System (PDS):** Planetary imagery, geological information, and mission observations.
- **NASA Earthdata:** Earth observation and environmental datasets.
- **USGS Astrogeology:** Planetary maps and cartographic resources.

These resources support the exploration and comparison of planetary environments. Dataset integration and availability depend on the implemented features.

## 🎯 Our Mission

To bridge the gap between Earth and space exploration through interactive technology, transforming complex planetary information into an accessible experience that inspires curiosity, discovery, and scientific learning.
